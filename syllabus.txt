## Course Completion

By the end of the course, I should be able to:

- Understand unfamiliar datasets systematically.
- Perform quantitative and visual EDA.
- Assess data quality and investigate anomalies.
- Analyze univariate, bivariate, and multivariate relationships.
- Recognize limitations, sampling issues, and misleading patterns.
- Build a reproducible EDA workflow.
- Produce a professional EDA report suitable for real-world clients.

The course ends with understanding and communicating the data,
not training a machine learning model.
